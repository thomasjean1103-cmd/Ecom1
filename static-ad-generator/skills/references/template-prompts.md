## Category: Headline Bold (Templates 1–8)

### Template 01 — hero-statement
**Ratio:** 1:1

A bold, high-impact static ad for {BRAND_NAME}. The product {PRODUCT_NAME} is displayed prominently in the center of the frame, product packaging clearly visible. Large {FONT_STYLE} typography overlaid reads: "{HEADLINE}" in {PRIMARY_COLOR}. Below it, smaller subtext reads: "{SUBHEADLINE}". A {SECONDARY_COLOR} CTA button at the bottom reads "{CTA}". Clean background, studio-lit, high-contrast composition. No people, product-first.
